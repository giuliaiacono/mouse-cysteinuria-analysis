Methaphlan taxonomic abundance analysis

MetaPhlAn uses (trimmed, host-filtered) reads aligned to a reference database of marker genes to assign taxonomy and estimate relative abundance of taxa. Taxonomic labels were converted to the GTDB r202 taxonomy to allow comparison with other analyses.

(MetaPhlAn version 4.0.6, vOct22_CHOCOPhlAnSGB_202212 database).

Beta diversity - Bray-Curtis PCoA

Bray-Curtis dissimilarity quantifies the difference between the composition of groups in the range 0 to 1, where 1 is most dissimilar. If both samples share the same taxa at the same relative abundance, their Bray-Curtis dissimilarity is 0. If they share no common taxa, they will have a dissimilarity of 1.

PERMANOVA

PERMANOVA can be used to test which taxa most contribute to the differences in overall relative abundance between Het and Homo (based on the centroid and dispersion of all taxonomic abundances at the Species level).

ANCOM-BC2 for differential abundance

ANCOM-BC2 fits linear mixed-models to estimate the log-fold change of taxa between groups, and includes terms to account for sampling fraction and per-taxa sequencing efficiency.

We drop the Abx condition - this is both not of interest for the main comparison, very different to the other conditions giving many ‘significant’ differences.

We exclude any taxa that aren’t detected in at least 90 % of samples.
